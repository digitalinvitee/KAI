Philosophy

Every detail has a purpose.

会 kai a meeting, a gathering

The same character ends ichigo ichie (一期一会), a phrase from the tea ceremony: one time, one meeting. No encounter happens twice.

Each evening at the counter happens once: between guest and kitchen, season and hand, rice and fish.

We cook what the week brings in. Every piece is cut and seasoned in front of you, and anything that does not serve the flavour stays off the plate.

Three principles

Season
The menu follows the market, not the other way round.
Precision
Rice at body temperature. Fish aged for days, not hours.
Hospitality
Ten seats, one counter, one conversation.
